Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Mooziac for macOS.

What is Mooziac?
Mooziac is an open-source, native macOS music player designed for modern Mac users. It lives in your menu bar and seamlessly integrates YouTube Music streaming, offline local audio playback (.mp3, .flac, .wav, .m4a), synchronized LRC lyrics, and haptic trackpad edge volume gestures.
How does Mooziac connect with YouTube Music?
Mooziac uses an isolated native WebKit session to connect securely with your YouTube Music account. You can access your Liked Songs, artist radio, and customized playlists without keeping an entire browser window open.
Can Mooziac play offline local music files?
Yes! Mooziac features a high-performance native CoreAudio engine that can play local audio files in formats like FLAC, MP3, WAV, and M4A. Simply drag and drop your music files or folders into the player.
How does trackpad edge volume control work?
Mooziac monitors multitouch input on the rightmost 1mm edge of your MacBook trackpad. Sliding your finger vertically along this edge gives you precision volume adjustments paired with tactile haptic feedback ticks.
Is Mooziac free and open source?
Yes, Mooziac is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. You can inspect the source code, contribute features, and build it yourself from the GitHub repository.
What are the macOS system requirements?
Mooziac requires macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later, including macOS 14 (Sonoma) and macOS 15 (Sequoia). It runs natively on both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel-based Macs.
