v1.0.1 Latest Release September 2026
Performance, Audio Streaming & Gesture Refinements
  • ✨
    Trackpad Edge Deadzone: Refined the edge touch trigger zone to a calibrated 1mm margin to prevent accidental volume triggers during system gestures and full-screen games.
  • ⚡
    YouTube Music Stream Resiliency: Added automated zero-drop audio stream recovery and background buffer warming for slow internet connections.
  • 📝
    Synced Lyrics HUD: Improved .lrc timestamp matching precision and added smooth auto-scrolling with current line spotlight highlighting.
  • 🎧
    High-Res Local Metadata: Enhanced ID3v2, Vorbis Comments, and embedded artwork extraction for FLAC, M4A, WAV, and MP3 files.
  • 🍏
    Universal Binary: Fully native performance on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs with sub-50MB RAM footprint.

v1.0.0 August 2026
Initial Public Release
  • 🚀
    Native Menu Bar Player: Tucked directly into the top macOS status bar with album artwork, playback transport, and seek controls.
  • 👆
    Trackpad Edge Volume Control: First-of-its-kind gesture allowing users to adjust system volume simply by sliding fingers along the trackpad's right edge.
  • 🎨
    Ambient Album Tinting: Live adaptive color grading for the menu bar and mini player based on active album artwork.
  • 💬
    Discord Rich Presence: Automatic status broadcasts showing currently playing track title, artist, and album artwork on Discord.
  • 🔒
    Zero Telemetry & Privacy First: Built with local-first architecture. No user data leaves your Mac, and no account logins are required.
