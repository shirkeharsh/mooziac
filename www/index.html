Engineered For macOS

Everything you love. Zero bloat.

Engineered natively with AppKit, WebKit, and CoreAudio for blistering speed and minimal battery footprint.

Lives in Menu Bar

Tucked neatly into the macOS status bar. Access your entire queue without switching spaces or juggling heavy desktop windows.

YouTube Music Sync

Sign in securely with standard Google session cookies. Sync all your Liked Songs, artist albums, and custom playlists instantly.

Trackpad Edge Gestures

Slide your finger along the far-right 1mm edge of your MacBook trackpad to adjust volume with realistic tactile haptic feedback.

Synchronized Lyrics

Real-time scrolling lyrics synced directly with your tracks via LRCLib and Lyrics.ovh for an effortless sing-along experience.

Discord Rich Presence

Share your music live on Discord. Displays your currently playing song, artist, album artwork, and time remaining automatically.

100% Private & Local

Zero tracking SDKs, zero ads, and zero server telemetry. Your favorites and listening history stay locked in your local SQLite database.
